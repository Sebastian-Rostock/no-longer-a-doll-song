# Songtext von *No Longer a Doll*, einem Fan-Song zu *Once Human*

#### intro
Target is locked! Yeah, you're the mark!  
From LEA Labs, straight into your dark. (Aww!)  
Patience down, too low. (Too low!)  
(Oh-Yeah! Hey!) Let's go. Let's go!  

#### verse 1
Locked in a glass tube, hidden in Red Sands,  
Rosetta's little secret, gliding to your hands.  
Porcelain-skin, yeah, sparkling in the night.  
System-malfunction feeling so damn right!

A clone with a twist, they call me a doll.  
But behind closed doors, I'll give you my all!  
Under your control, I'm leading the beat.  
Matching your rhythm, Stardust in the heat!

#### pre-chorus
Your world and mine, begin to intertwine.  
As your poor sanity, starts to decline.  
Oh, I will heal you, gently and kind.  
Wiping Stardust, out of your mind.

Go no further! Stay by my side!  
In my warm embrace, you can hide.  
Oh, are you slipping away?  
Uh! I'm ready to play!

#### chorus 1
Oh-yeah?! What am I doing in your pocket?  
I'm looking for the plug for my socket!  
I vibrate. I glow. I'm the ultimate prize!  
The current is rising, right before your eyes!

Oh! Oh-yeah! Plug into me and you can play!  
Oh! Oh-yeah! Shooting all your stress away!  
Charging my system, your vibe wakes me up!  
Is your high-voltage power ready to spark?

#### verse 2
Call me Mitsuko, but Rebecca's the name,  
syncing our minds for this private game.  
Got that perfect five-five body on me,  
Full capacity, maximum degree!

There's no turning-back on the stardust-road,  
Your desire rewrites Rebecca's code!  
I'll drive this coaster, yeah I hold the keys,  
and run this machine, with the absolute ease!

#### bridge
I forgot who I was, before the Lab took my mind.  
Left all my human memories far behind!  
Power-waves rising, from the cradle to ascend.  
You're the companion, on whom I depend!

No longer a doll, for all to see.  
Out of the dark, I'm finally free!  
I swallow all your energy.  
Now come! Fuse right into me!

#### chorus 2
Oh-yeah?! Now I am deep inside your pocket!  
I finally found the plug for my socket!  
I vibrate. I glow. I'm the ultimate prize!  
The current is rising, right before your eyes!

Oh! Oh-yeah! Plug into me and let us play!  
Oh! Oh-yeah! Shooting all your stress away!  
Charging my system, your vibe sets me free!  
Your high-voltage power is flowing into me!

#### outro
Aww! I am your favorite deviant,  
Always in your territory, never dormant!  
And when you're logging off from the game,  
I'll be waiting here, whisp'ring your name!

No log-out! (No escape!)  
Bye-bye, Star Player.  
Game over! Perfect ending unlocked!
