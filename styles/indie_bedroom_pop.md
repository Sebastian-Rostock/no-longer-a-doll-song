## Indie Bedroom-Pop

&#128286; **Hinweis zum Jugendschutz:** Bitte beachte die [Altersbeschränkungen im Hauptdokument](../README.md#cw).

**Stimmung & Bezug zur Story:**  
Nahbar, verletzlich, melancholisch und ungeschminkt. Inspiriert vom *Once Human*-Radiosender **97.7 Hometown FM** zieht dieser Stil alle elektronischen Effekte ab: Rebecca sitzt nach dem Ausloggen allein im Raum und flüstert den Namen des Spielers (*Always in your territory, never dormant / I'll be waiting here, whisp'ring your name*).

## Tracks & Style-Prompts

#### Variante 01

[➜ 19 - No Longer a Doll (Indie Bedroom-Pop)](https://suno.com)

````text
Intimate indie bedroom-pop, warm clean electric guitar with soft chorus and tape warble, stripped-back dusty lo-fi drum groove, warm analog bass; close-mic breathy female vocals, fragile and conversational, unpolished emotional honesty, gentle harmonies on the chorus, 95 BPM, G major
````

#### Variante 02

[➜ 19_02 - No Longer a Doll (Indie Bedroom-Pop)](https://suno.com)

````text
Melancholic indie pop, dreamy nostalgic guitar arpeggios, soft brushed snare and warm upright bass, late-night radio static ambience; tender female vocals in a warm mid-range, hushed intimacy with slight vocal crack on emotional lines, bittersweet uplifting chorus, 98 BPM, E major
````
